# Contributing

**This project is not accepting external contributions at this time.**

It's shared publicly so you're welcome to **use it, clone it, and adapt it** for your
own needs under the [MIT License](LICENSE). However, the maintainer is not currently
reviewing or merging pull requests, and issues are turned off.

If you build something useful on top of it, feel free to **fork** and maintain your own
copy — that's fully allowed by the license.

This may change in the future. Thanks for understanding.
